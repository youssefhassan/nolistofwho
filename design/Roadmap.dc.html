The counter: from one strike to many counters

Dates after Nov 25 are proposals. Each milestone is tied to an action that is already announced.
MVP · Wed Nov 25
One strike, counted
  • The Attention Strike and "I deleted my Amazon account"
  • The ticket: email and word never leave the phone
  • Two numbers: in, did it
  • Areas as a list, 10 or more only
  • What Amazon lost: addresses, payment methods
  • The receipt, the share image
  • Next action, one calendar for all dates
  • Fake entries removed: n
  • Honest footer, Tor, English
Proves: a count with no list of who can be built and can hold on the day.
M1 · Sun Dec 13
Steps and German
  • Steps with no date: cancelled Prime, requested my data, less personalised ads, deleted Facebook
  • German
  • "Remind me every week": a repeating calendar entry made on the phone
  • Fixes from the privacy review
Tied to: the first Sunday off X. Steps are what people already send screenshots of.
M2 · Sun Jan 3
The map and the neighbourhood
  • The map of action: your area close up, the world far away
  • Map files served by us, no Google or Mapbox
  • A goal per area: "Kreuzberg 36: 500 people"
  • Goals belong to an area, never to a person
Tied to: the second Sunday off X. Needs the Nov 27 numbers to have something to draw.
M3 · Wed Jan 20
Shops and groups, by name
  • A second number anyone can check: "4,312 people, 12 shops"
  • Shops and organisations join in public, with a name and a link
  • A count badge for their own page, with no tracking
Tied to: leaving X together. The first step toward the local-shop matcher.
M4 · February 2027
Anyone can call an action
  • A group proposes an action; Youssef approves; it gets a page, a count and a calendar entry
  • Installable page, no app store
  • Email reminders with the plain warning, only if still wanted
Tied to: the Meta actions. Other groups get a count without keeping a list.
M5 · Spring 2027
Many counters, one number
  • The recipe: run your own counter on your own machine
  • Each counter publishes its totals in a small open file; one page adds them up
  • Arabic, right to left
Recipes, not platforms: no single server to pressure or to trust.
Never in any milestone: accounts, names, leaderboards of people, ads, payments No list of who
